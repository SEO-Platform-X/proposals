Steve's bio

Steve Lee is the founder and CEO of Local AI Registry. He is a former Google Search and Microsoft executive with more than 20 years in digital, and a former aesthetics industry executive at Revance and Evolus. He also owns a med spa.

About Local AI Registry

Local AI Registry is a public record of what AI assistants say about local businesses. It shows businesses, from med spas to plastic surgery practices, what AI gets right and wrong about them, and gets accurate information into AI answers.
