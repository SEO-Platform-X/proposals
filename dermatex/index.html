Your website, read the way AI reads it

AI doesn’t see your beautiful results or feel the experience in your studio. It reads words and code. Here’s what it finds on dermatexhair.com today.

We don’t recommend patching this site. It needs to be rebuilt around one clear story, which is why we’re offering the rebuild at our cost.

It never says what you actually do

Your homepage“all proven non-surgical hair restoration and hair loss treatment solutions that really work”

There isn’t one sentence explaining what a hair system is, what it’s made of, how it attaches, how long it lasts, what it costs, or who it’s right for. AI can’t recommend what it can’t describe.

It sells a service you don’t offer

Two full pages and both menusScalp Micropigmentation (SMP) appears under Men’s Solutions, Women’s Solutions, the footer, and on two separate URLs.

You’ve told us SMP isn’t something Dermatex does. Every one of those pages teaches AI the wrong thing about you, and anyone who books for it is a wasted consult.

Google’s preview of your homepage is button text

Your homepage description, as Google and AI see it“Life-Changing Results Restore Your Confidence … SCHEDULE A CONSULTATION”

This is the first summary search engines and AI tools read. It names no service, no city, and no reason to choose you.

Your age changes depending on the page

Four different answers“over 32 years” on the homepage, “over 30 years” and “30+ Years” elsewhere, and press releases say founded in 1987, which would make it 39.

AI trusts facts that agree across sources. When your own sources disagree, AI hedges or leaves you out. Thirty-nine years is a powerful “first and longest” story you aren’t telling.

So does your name, phone and home base

What AI has to reconcile“Hair Replacement” in the page title, “Hair Restoration Orange County CA” as the site name, “Laguna Hills” in the footer. Two different Facebook pages linked. Booking on a separate domain. The San Diego button sends people to a different brand, Fidenza Hair. An older press release lists a different phone number.

AI connects facts about a business by matching name, address and phone. Every variation splits Dermatex into weaker, partial versions of itself.

Duplicate pages compete against each other

Same topic, different URLsTwo SMP pages, two women’s hair loss pages, two About pages. The menu links to one version and the footer links to another, and both www and non-www addresses are ranking.

Google splits your authority across the copies and usually shows the weaker one. You’re competing with yourself.

Your best proof is invisible to AI

Homepage, “Real People. Real Results.”Four videos, and no before-and-after photos with a written explanation of what was done.

Your results are your strongest sales tool. AI can’t watch a video or judge a photo it has no description for, so right now your proof counts for nothing in AI answers.

Small things AI copies word for word

“Orange Country” in the booking box on the SMP page. “califfornia” in image descriptions. A tiny logo as the preview image when pages are shared. Each one is minor; together they read as a site nobody is watching.

You’re already paying for “SEO”

The footer credits a web design and SEO firm. The result today is 7 ranking keywords and about 45 visits a month. That’s the gap we’re here to close.
