Your website, read the way a US buyer reads it

A med spa owner, a dermatologist or a plastic surgeon spends about two minutes deciding whether Nova is a brand they can trust with their patients. Here’s what they find.

We don’t recommend patching this site. It needs to be rebuilt for American clinic owners around one clear story: the same results as the big brands, for a lot less.

It introduces Nova as a factory

About page“An integrated manufacturer specializing in research, production, sales, and service, with over 5,000 square meters of modern facilities … a factory pass rate of 97.8%.”

American buyers want a brand with US support, not a factory floor measured in square meters. And a 97.8% pass rate means about 1 in 45 machines fails testing. That line costs sales.

The $65,000 laser page looks like a reseller listing

AI-Esthetician product page34 photos, including files named “WechatIMG14” and “WechatIMG41,” and phone screenshots dated 1970. “Add to cart.” “Low stock: 10 left.” “Free shipping on all orders.”

Big equipment purchases need a demo, financing, training and a warranty. A shopping cart button tells a buyer this is a marketplace product.

There’s no proof that American clinics use Nova

Homepage and product pages20 reviews with no names, a “20k+ Patient Worldwide” counter, and no doctor, nurse injector or clinic named anywhere.

Clinic owners buy what respected peers use. Nova already pays doctors and nurses to refer sales. Those same people, shown publicly, are the strongest sales tool Nova has.

It borrows other brands’ names

Product names and copy“Hydrafacial SkinCare,” “EMSCULPT (HIFEM),” “Endospheres therapy.” These are trademarks owned by other companies.

This is a legal risk, and to a buyer it reads as a knockoff of the real thing, which is exactly the impression Nova needs to escape.

FDA claims a US buyer can’t verify

About page“Certified by CE, FDA, ISO 13485.” No clearance numbers anywhere. HIFU pages also promote “vaginal tightening.”

The FDA clears devices; it doesn’t certify them, and buyers and their medical directors look up the 510(k) number. The FDA has also warned that no energy device is cleared for vaginal rejuvenation. Physicians read both as red flags.

It talks to patients, not clinic owners

Homepage“Make an Appointment.” “Say goodbye to stubborn breakouts.” Clinic hours, and “Sunday: Closed (Emergency Open 24/7).”

A buyer can’t tell whether Nova sells machines or runs a med spa. The site has to speak to the person signing a $50,000 check.

Details that don’t match

Same product page, same footerDelivery shows “12 to 26 days” and “5 to 7 days.” The footer shows a 718 phone number that dials a 315 number. A laser page includes a Cold Plasma section copied from another product. Pacer One and Pacer One Pro link to each other’s pages.

Each one is small. Together they tell a buyer that nobody is minding the store.

Reads as translated, not written for Americans

“Aadvanced,” “Ai Skin,” a scrolling “Live without regrets” banner, the privacy policy listed under “Departments,” a link named “dz-skin-analyzer,” and a logo file named “Picsart AI Image Enhancer.”

The name and social profiles look unfinished

“Nova Skincare Tech” on a .tech address sounds like a face cream. Facebook still uses an auto-generated “profile.php?id=” address, and the YouTube handle is “@NovaSkin-f6t.”

So what are SEO and GEO?

Both make Nova show up when a clinic owner is already shopping. SEO gets Nova onto Google’s list. GEO gets Nova named when someone asks ChatGPT. Neither one makes a buyer trust what they find when they arrive.

SEO

Search engine optimization

A buyer types “morpheus8 machine cost” into Google. SEO puts a Nova comparison page on page one, right when they flinch at the price.

  • Competitor “alternative to” pages
  • Product pages built around real searches
  • Technical fixes and speed
GEO

Generative engine optimization

A buyer asks ChatGPT, “What’s a cheaper alternative to Emsculpt for my med spa?” GEO makes Nova one of the names in the answer.

  • Clear facts AI can quote: specs, prices, FDA numbers
  • Named doctors and clinics AI can verify
  • Mentions on trusted industry sites

Think of a store on a busy street. SEO and GEO put up the sign and pave the road. If the store looks unsafe from the doorway, more foot traffic just means more people walking back out.
