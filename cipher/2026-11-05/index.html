Five years of Cipher sales

One month compared with one other month can mislead. Here is every month since the store opened, so the ups and downs sit in their real context.

Online retail sales, every month since 2022

Source: Cipher WooCommerce orders. Completed retail orders only. Wholesale accounts, distributors and any single order of $2,000 or 50+ items are left out. November 2026 is not shown because only 4 days are in.

Spring 2024 surge. Sales jumped to $75,844 in April 2024, then cooled month after month for the next two years.

The slide before us. January to May 2026 averaged $15,374 a month, 28% below 2025. The drop started months before we arrived.

The turn. September broke the slide. October more than doubled September and topped every month since May 2024.

We turned a two-year slide around

The gray line is where Cipher's sales were heading. From June 2024 to May 2026 they fell about 3% every month. The dashed part shows where that slide would have taken October. The blue line is what actually happened.

If the slide had continued, October would have been about $13,200. It was $45,287. From June to October, Cipher sold $36,761 more than the old trend pointed to.

Trend line: the best fit through monthly retail sales from June 2024 to May 2026, extended forward at the same rate.

Is June always a slow month?

Each square is one month. Darker blue means more sales compared with the rest of that year, so each row is judged against itself.

Weaker month for that yearStronger month for that year
No. June was the 3rd best month in 2022, 9th in 2023, 3rd in 2024 and 6th in 2025. There is no regular summer slump in Cipher's history.

What June 2026 really was

June 2026 was the lowest month since February 2024. But it was 19% below the January to May pace, not a fall off a cliff. It was the bottom of a slide that had already taken 28% off sales before launch.

The market is shrinking too

Fewer people search for pipes every year. US searches for "one hitter" are down about 32% since early 2022. Cipher's October growth happened while its market got smaller.

Source: Ahrefs, monthly US Google searches.

Cipher vs. its market

Both lines start at 100, the average of the six months before we started. The gray line is how many people search Google for Cipher's product types each month. The blue line is Cipher's sales.

Before us: Cipher was falling faster than its market. In January to May 2026, searches for its product types held steady, while Cipher's sales fell 28%.

Since us: the market slipped another 9%. Cipher's sales went the other way, up 166%.

Market line: average of monthly US Google searches for "one hitter", "weed pipe", "dry herb vape" and "bong" (Ahrefs).

Google: before and after

Before the rebuild, Cipher's standing with Google had been stuck for a year. Since launch it has climbed fast.

Website strength score

What it means. Ahrefs rates every website from 0 to 100 based on who links to it. Higher scores rank more easily. Cipher sat between 18 and 22 for all of 2025.

Websites that link to Cipher

What it means. Each link is like a recommendation. Google counts them as votes of trust. Cipher has three times as many as it did at launch.
