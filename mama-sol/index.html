What the published research shows

Every ingredient was chosen from peer-reviewed research. These are findings about the ingredients and approach, not claims about Mama Sol's finished formulas.

Skin Pharmacology & Physiology · 2007 · Randomized, vehicle-controlled

Ectoin measurably improved skin hydration in a controlled human trial.

104women in a randomized trial of a 2% ectoin emulsion against a placebo cream
Why it mattersEctoin is in every FACEBRELLA™ formula. It is the strongest human evidence behind “supports underneath.”
Skin Pharmacology & Physiology · 2009 · Human skin study

A topical green tea antioxidant reached the living layers of human skin.

0.54µg/cm² of EGCG measured in the viable epidermis of human skin
Why it mattersSkincare does its work in the living epidermis. Certain topical antioxidants can get there.
Journal of Investigative Dermatology · 2003

Sunscreen alone did not fully stop UVA-induced free radicals in skin.

SPFmeasures sunburn protection. It does not measure every effect of daily exposure.
Why it mattersThe reason a daily SPF should do more than block, and the case for adding antioxidants.

Zinc oxide stays on the skin's surface, even after repeated application.
